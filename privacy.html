  • To provide your membership, including giving and removing access to Telegram channels, and answering support requests. Basis: performance of our contract with you.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run the Service:

  • GitHub Pages: website hosting, which may log visitor IP addresses for security.

We may also disclose data if required by law or to protect our rights. Some of these providers are based outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Our website does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. Whop's checkout pages and Telegram use their own cookies under their own policies. If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy and ask for consent where the law requires it.

We keep membership data for as long as you are a member and for up to 7 years afterwards for tax and accounting purposes. Support messages are kept for up to 2 years. Data we no longer need is deleted or anonymised.

If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority, such as the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos in Spain or the ICO in the UK.

We use reputable providers and limit access to member data to people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
